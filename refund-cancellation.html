Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: July 28, 2026

This Refund and Cancellation Policy explains how refunds, cancellations, and subscription terminations are handled by Majaliwa Tech Ltd ("we", "our", or "us"). It applies to all software, digital products, subscription services, and related offerings provided by the company, including products operated under our brands.

1. Digital Products and Services

Our products primarily consist of digital software and online services. Because these services become available immediately after purchase or activation, payments are generally non-refundable unless required by applicable law.

2. Subscription Services

If you subscribe to one of our recurring services:

  • You may cancel your subscription at any time.
  • Your subscription will remain active until the end of your current billing period.
  • Cancellation prevents future charges but does not automatically generate a refund for payments already made.

3. Refund Eligibility

Refund requests may be considered under the following circumstances:

  • You were charged more than once for the same purchase.
  • You were charged due to a billing error.
  • You were unable to access the purchased service because of a verified technical issue caused by us.
  • Applicable consumer protection laws require a refund.

Each request is reviewed individually, and supporting information may be requested.

4. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds are generally not available for:

  • Change of mind after purchase.
  • Failure to use or fully utilise the purchased service.
  • Compatibility issues caused by unsupported devices or software.
  • Service interruptions caused by third-party providers beyond our control.
  • Violations of our Terms of Service resulting in account suspension or termination.

5. Cancellation by Majaliwa Tech Ltd

We reserve the right to suspend or terminate services where required to protect our platform, comply with legal obligations, investigate fraud, or enforce our Terms of Service.

Where appropriate, any refund will be determined based on the circumstances and applicable laws.

6. Processing Refunds

Approved refunds will be issued using the original payment method whenever possible. Processing times vary depending on your payment provider and may take several business days before the funds appear in your account.

7. Contact Us

If you believe you qualify for a refund or have questions regarding this policy, please contact us and include your purchase details, payment reference, and a description of the issue.
